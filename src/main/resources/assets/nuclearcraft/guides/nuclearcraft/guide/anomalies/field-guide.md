# Anomaly Field Guide

**Anomalies** are stationary environmental hazards found only in the **Wasteland**. They are not animals and they are not interested in you personally — they simply do what they do to anything that wanders inside their radius.

## What you need to know

- **You cannot kill them.** Weapons, explosions, fall damage, fire — none of it registers. Each anomaly is removed only by its own specific *counterplay* (see Anomaly Types). A radioactive one is never removed at all.
- **They never move on or despawn.** Placement is fixed per world seed: every anomaly sits where the Zone decided it would sit, and once you clear one it stays cleared.
- **They announce themselves.** Each variant has its own ambient sound, particle haze, and shimmer. If the air is humming, crackling, or warping — stop walking.
- **Each has a radius.** The effect reaches well past the visible core. Scout the edge before you commit.

## Spoils of the Zone

Clearing an anomaly through its counterplay drops **Resonite Shards** — the raw material for resonite crystals (see Shards & Crystals). The reward survives the collapse blast, so don't be shy about it. Radioactive anomalies have no counterplay and therefore drop nothing; log them, route around them, move on.

(The Operator's Handbook reminds you that "route around them" is a perfectly honorable expedition outcome.)
