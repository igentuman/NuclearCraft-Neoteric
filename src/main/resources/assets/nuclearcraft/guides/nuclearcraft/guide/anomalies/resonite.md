## Resonite Shard

A **Resonite Shard** is what every counterplayed anomaly leaves behind (radioactive ones excepted — they never resolve, so they never pay out). Plain on its own, but the only feedstock for crystals.

## Resonite Crystal

Craft nine shards in a 3×3 to press a single raw **Resonite Crystal**. Raw, it does nothing — no buff, no power. Its potential is sealed until it is analyzed.

## Analysis

Run a raw crystal through an **Analyzer**. Analysis rolls two things and writes them into the crystal for good:

- A **grade** — Common, Rare, Epic, or Legendary. Higher grades are far rarer.
- A **resonance** — one beneficial effect, drawn at random from the full beneficial pool (including radiation resistance, a vitality boost, and a quickdraw boost).

Re-analyzing an already-analyzed crystal is safe: it keeps its existing grade and resonance rather than rerolling. (The certification stamp does not come off.)

## Carrying an analyzed crystal

An analyzed crystal works passively while it is **in your inventory or worn in a Curios slot** — no need to hold it:

- **Resonance buff** — you continuously gain the crystal's effect. The grade sets the strength: a higher grade means a stronger effect. Carry several with the *same* resonance and only the strongest applies; different resonances all stack with each other.
- **Infinite power cell** — the crystal acts as a bottomless FE battery that never drains. Higher grades output more FE per tick (Common is modest; Legendary is enormous). Slot it into any machine that pulls power from its inventory.

Legendary crystals carry a golden sheen — and, for certain resonances, a line in the logbook crediting the operator who first walked one out of the Zone.
