# Anomaly Types

Six known variants. Each has exactly one way out.

## Gravitational Anomaly

Drags everything within reach toward its core, flings the unlucky skyward, and quietly swallows loose blocks and dropped items. The more it eats, the larger and hungrier it grows.

**Counterplay:** feed it. Once it has absorbed enough mass it collapses in a violent explosion and is gone. Or simply walk out of its pull — patience is free, respawns are not.

## Electrical Anomaly

Lashes nearby creatures with arc lightning on a cycle. The bolts are showy; the damage is real.

**Counterplay:** set a battery (any block with a large empty energy buffer) beside it. The anomaly will dump its charge into the buffer and dissipate. The bigger the empty buffer, the surer the discharge.

## Radioactive Anomaly

Bathes the area in a heavy dose. Players accumulate radiation; other creatures simply cook.

**Counterplay:** none. This one is permanent. Mark it, shield up, and keep your distance — or let resonite handle the dose (see Shards & Crystals).

## Thermal Anomaly

Sets everything in range alight and periodically throws off a small explosion. May ignite the landscape if your server allows it.

**Counterplay:** drown it. Pour enough water that several source blocks sit right against the core and the flames go out for good.

## Psi Anomaly

Floods the minds of nearby **players** with blindness, nausea, weakness, and a leaden slowness. It also conjures vexes to keep you company. Other creatures are unaffected; the Zone finds them uninteresting.

**Counterplay:** a direct hit from the **Q36 Quantite Disruptor**. Aim true — the splash does not count.

## Spatial Anomaly

Hovers above the surface, blinking from spot to spot, and hurls anything that strays too close to a random location up to a hundred blocks away. Do not approach. (Per Directive 12-B, which exists because someone did.)

**Counterplay:** a direct hit from the **Q36 Quantite Disruptor** — if you can land one on a target that refuses to hold still.
