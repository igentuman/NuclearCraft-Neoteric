# Generator Machines

These machines generate energy in relatively small quantities, but they are also simple to operate. Ideal for outposts where the grid is more of a rumor than a guarantee.

## RTGs

<Row>
  <BlockImage id="americium_rtg" />
  <BlockImage id="californium_rtg" />
  <BlockImage id="plutonium_rtg" />
  <BlockImage id="uranium_rtg" />

An **RTG** generates energy from the decay of its radioactive payload. Output is steady, modest, and continues whether you are paying attention or not.
