# Processing Machines

These machines process materials, usually by the use of energy, to convert materials from one type to another.

## Manufactory

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  <BlockImage id="manufactory" />
</Column>

The **Manufactory** is a machine that has many uses, mainly in processing materials.

## Isotope Separator

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  <BlockImage id="isotope_separator" />
</Column>

The **Separator** separates materials into their constituent parts.

## Decay Hastener

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  <BlockImage id="decay_hastener" />
</Column>

The **Decay Hastener** forces radioactive materials to decay, resulting in the formation of a different isotope or material. Radiation is released during the decay process.

## Fuel Reprocessor

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  <BlockImage id="fuel_reprocessor" />
</Column>

The **Fuel Reprocessor** extracts materials from depleted fuel, allowing for the isolation of useful material from depleted fuel.

## Alloy Smelter

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  <BlockImage id="alloy_smelter" />
</Column>

The **Alloy Smelter** combines base metals together into alloys.

## Fluid Infuser

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  <BlockImage id="fluid_infuser" />
</Column>

The **Fluid Infuser** adds fluids to materials.

## Melter

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  <BlockImage id="melter" />
</Column>

The **Melter** melts down materials into a liquid form.

## Supercooler

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  <BlockImage id="supercooler" />
</Column>

The **Supercooler** lowers the temperature of fluids.

## Electrolyzer

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  <BlockImage id="electrolyzer" />
</Column>

The **Electrolyzer** splits fluids into their constituent parts.

## Assembler

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  <BlockImage id="assembler" />
</Column>

The **Assembler** combines components into a complex product.

## Ingot Former

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  <BlockImage id="ingot_former" />
</Column>

The **Ingot Former** forms molten materials into ingots or gems.

## Pressurizer

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  <BlockImage id="pressurizer" />
</Column>

The **Pressurizer** processes materials under immense pressure.

## Chemical Reactor

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  <BlockImage id="chemical_reactor" />
</Column>

The **Chemical Reactor** houses reactions between fluids, allowing for chemical reactions to occur.

## Crystallizer

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  <BlockImage id="crystallizer" />
</Column>

The **Crystallizer** precipitates solids from a fluid.

## Fluid Enricher

  <BlockImage id="fluid_enricher" />

## Fluid Extractor

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  <BlockImage id="extractor" />

## Centrifuge

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  <BlockImage id="centrifuge" />
