# Q36 Quantite Disruptor

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  <ItemImage id="q36_quantite_disruptor" />
</Column>

The **Q36 Quantite Disruptor** is a high-energy directed weapon. Discharges a quantite beam that disassembles matter at the molecular level. Consumes internal power per shot - keep a charger nearby, or carry spares. (Marketing brochure omits the cleanup requirements.)
